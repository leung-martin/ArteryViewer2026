Interactive 3D visualization of facial arteries overlaid on a translucent nose & mouth mesh.

Select an artery to adjust its diameter, length (grows from center outward), and Z position (depth offset). Range labels show ± adjustable extent from the anatomical default.

Built with Three.js r128  ·  Vanilla JS  ·  v1.2
